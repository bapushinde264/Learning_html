Water (H₂O) is a clear, colorless, and odorless liquid that is essential for all known forms of life. Each water molecule is made up of two hydrogen atoms and one oxygen atom. It covers about 71% of Earth's surface and is found in oceans, rivers, lakes, glaciers, and the atmosphere. Water is important for drinking, agriculture, cooking, cleaning, and many industrial processes. It also helps regulate Earth’s temperature and supports plants, animals, and humans.
